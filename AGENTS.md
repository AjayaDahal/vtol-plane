# Notes for agents

- When the user starts or asks about printing, read the "Reminders for print time" section in
  `tracker.md` and remind them of every unchecked item before they print the full set.
- Pipeline and slicer settings: `SLICING.md`. Design numbers come from `scripts/mass_balance.py`.
  Design, assembly order and hand-drilled holes: `DESIGN.md`.
- After any change to `scripts/` or `slicer_profiles/`, run `python scripts/build_all.py` (rebuild,
  re-slice, verify). `scripts/check_parts.py` must pass before anything is printed.
- Every print job is a 3MF project (`3mf/`, the owner opens it in Anycubic Slicer Next to send
  it to the printer) and G-code (`gcode/`, same folders and names). `slice_all.py` writes the 3MF
  from the STLs and profiles, then slices that 3MF on its own, so both always come from one run;
  never edit either by hand. The gate checks both (stamp, presets, no strays).
- The owner prints from two complete projects, `3mf/complete_project_kobra_x_lwpla.3mf` and
  `3mf/complete_project_kobra_s1_petg.3mf`: one named plate per job, in print order.
  `scripts/make_projects.py` assembles them from the job 3MFs (`slice_all.py` and `build_all.py`
  run it; `--verify`, in `build_all.py`, slices every plate again against its job's G-code). The
  gate checks them file for file. The slicer's CLI crashes on any plate name, so verification
  slices a copy with the names blanked; keep plate names out of the job 3MFs.
- Slicer: Anycubic Slicer Next 2.0.0.3. If it updates, re-slice everything and diff the output
  before trusting it: 2.0 changed the CLI's default plate type, its auto brim and its vase-mode
  object labels (`REPORT.md`, round 11). The machine profiles come from the installed slicer's
  presets (`make_slicer_profiles.py`). `scripts/audit_print.py` (needs the slicer, not in the
  gate) shows what supports the slicer itself would add and how slender each part stands; after
  a PETG geometry or profile change, run it with `--render` to redraw `renders/51`.
- The slice previews (`renders/48`-`50`, `preview_slices.py`) are drawn from the G-code by
  `build_all.py` and `slice_all.py`; the gate checks they show the current plates (labels
  included, so after changing `HOLD` or a plate's name in `make_projects.py`, run
  `preview_slices.py`). Look at them
  after a slicing change: they are how the "hole edges in mid-air" fault was found (`REPORT.md`
  11.9), which the gate's over-air check now catches.
- Brims and other per-part slicer settings live in the print table (`make_print_ready.py`,
  `brim` and `slicer`); the PETG profile prints no brim. `slice_all.py` writes them onto each
  part's object in its 3MF, and the gate checks the 3MFs carry exactly those. It also requires a
  brim on exactly the PETG parts standing taller than the root of their first-layer contact area,
  so a geometry change can add or drop one there.
- The print table and totals in `tracker.md` are generated from the sliced files
  (`scripts/make_tracker.py`, run by `build_all.py` and `slice_all.py` after slicing). Edit only
  the Status column, the notes in brackets after a part's name and the text around the tables;
  the gate fails when the table is stale.
- PETG prints as plates on the S1 (`scripts/plates.py`): every PETG copy must sit on exactly one
  plate, so a new or changed PETG part (or `qty`) needs its plate entry too; the gate checks
  this and the layouts. LW-PLA vase parts cannot share a bed. Printer split, one material per
  printer (the owner's choice: one LW-PLA spool, one flow calibration): the X prints every
  LW-PLA skin, the S1 all the PETG (PETG warps on the open-frame X). Keep it that way; the X has
  the long queue (about 49 h against 16 h, `tracker.md` totals).
- When a finding is fixed or a new one is found, update its status in `REPORT.md` (the
  "Current status" section and the round tables) in the same commit. Never leave a fixed
  finding marked open, or an open one marked fixed.
- Renders: after a geometry change, run `python scripts/make_viewer.py` and
  `python scripts/serve_viewer.py`, then open `http://127.0.0.1:28765/viewer/?capture=1` to
  re-render `renders/*.png` and the README's animations (`renders/anim_*.gif`; the server
  writes them with Pillow), so the pictures match the files. `?capture=gif` redoes only the
  animations. `renders/vtol_plane.stl` and `renders/print_schedule.svg` are generated by the
  build (`make_viewer.py`, `make_tracker.py`); the gate checks the chart.
- `README.md` is the front page: keep its figures rounded or pointing at `DESIGN.md` and
  `tracker.md`, which hold the exact ones. CI (`.github/workflows/gate.yml`) runs the gate on
  every push to `main`, on the committed files.
- Purchased equipment (battery, ESCs, Pixhawk, boards, GPS, servos, pitot) lives in
  `scripts/payload.py`: size and position of each item, and a sketch of the cable runs. Move
  equipment there, not in the docs; the mass balance, the gate's fit checks and the viewer all
  read it. The servo hatch frames (`servo_hatches()`) live there too.
- Nothing that may need replacing (battery, servos, motors, boards) may be glued in or sit
  behind a joint that has to be cut: the nose, the screwed servo hatches and the pods' plug
  pockets are the ways in (`DESIGN.md`, "Servicing"). The gate checks the hatches, the plug
  pockets and the battery-strap path.
- The hover margin is thin (front motors ~60 % of their near-empty thrust, the gate's limit):
  count every new gram in `mass_balance.py` and re-run the gate.
- The owner is moving to a 3S build (`PLAN_3S.md`, not built in yet): his own 3S packs, Cube
  Black, Power Brick Mini, 3DR GPS and Readytosky ESCs, with SunnySky X2814 900KV motors on
  12 in props. Until it is built in, PETG plates 6 and 7 stay on hold (`tracker.md`). To build
  it in, follow the plan's "Order of work" (tag `v0.2-4s`, branch `build-3s`, PR), then update
  this note and the hover-margin note above.
- The wing webs depend on two vase-profile settings, `slice_closing_radius` 0.01 and
  `detect_thin_wall` 0 (`SLICING.md`), and the PETG over-air check on `detect_overhang_wall` 1.
  Do not let a preset update reset them; the gate reads
  them, the bed temperatures and the other key settings back from every G-code file. The G-code
  stamp only covers the STL, so after any profile change re-slice everything.
